IKEA web 3D model, taken apart

Part by part

IKEA ships its web models as a single merged mesh. Splitting it wherever the geometry is physically disconnected (split_parts.py) recovers the separate parts, including hardware hidden inside. Pull the slider to explode the chair; hover or click a part to identify it.

Parts

Grouped by role. Numbers are the part's bounding size in cm (W × H × D).

Parts are found by geometry, not from IKEA's parts list: any piece not connected to another becomes a part. Names follow its size and position ("left/right" as seen from the front). Glued or merged pieces would stay together.
